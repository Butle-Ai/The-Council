## Mode: LONG-TERM VENTURE

Judge this as a **long-term business**: something a small founding team would commit several years to, possibly raising money, aiming to build something large and lasting.

What matters most:
- **Size of the prize.** How big can this get in 3-5+ years?
- **Defensibility.** What stops competitors from copying it once it works?
- **Scalability.** Can it grow without costs growing just as fast?
- **Unit economics** at scale.
- **Timing.** Why is now the right moment?
- **Capital and risk.** How much money and time does it take before it's clearly working?

Seat notes:
- Believer: "best case" means year 1, year 3, and year 5.
- Skeptic: focus on competition from funded companies, defensibility, and what kills startups of this type.
- Investor: say whether this is venture-scale or a good-but-smaller business, and what you'd need to see before investing.
- Judge, use this scorecard: Customer demand / pain | Market size | Competition / differentiation | Feasibility | Unit economics | Defensibility | Risk (10 = low risk) | Timing | Overall value score.
