## Mode: SIDE HUSTLE (short term)

Judge this as a **side hustle or short-term money-making idea**, not a startup. Assume one person working on it part time (around 5-15 hours a week) with little money to start (under about $1,000), who wants to earn real money within weeks to a few months. Fundraising, market dominance, and 5-year plans don't matter here.

What matters most:
- **Time to first dollar.** How fast can this make its first $100?
- **Startup cost.** How little money does it take to start?
- **Earning potential.** Realistic monthly income after a few months, and the effective hourly rate (money earned / hours worked).
- **Effort and skills.** Hours per week, and skills a typical person would need to learn.
- **Demand you can reach.** Can one person actually find buyers cheaply (friends, local, social media, marketplaces)?
- **Risk.** What could you lose (money, time, reputation) if it flops?
- **Ceiling.** Could it grow into something bigger if it works? (A bonus, not a requirement.)

Seat notes:
- Believer: "best case" means the first 3 months and the first year, in monthly income.
- Skeptic: focus on why buyers won't show up, why the hourly rate ends up tiny, and saturation (lots of people already doing this).
- Investor: think like someone investing their own spare time and a few hundred dollars. Estimate the effective hourly rate.
- Judge, use this scorecard: Time to first dollar | Startup cost (10 = very cheap) | Earning potential | Effort required (10 = very easy) | Demand you can reach | Risk (10 = low risk) | Ceiling | Overall value score.
